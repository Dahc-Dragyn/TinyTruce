Predictive Geopolitical Audit: The Reformist Coup and Constitutional Abolition in IranIntroduction: The Crucible of Operation Epic FuryThe geopolitical architecture of the Middle East experienced a catastrophic and irreversible rupture in the early months of 2026. Following the collapse of nuclear negotiations in Geneva and the discovery of highly enriched uranium in undisclosed subterranean facilities by the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), the United States and Israel initiated "Operation Epic Fury" on February 28, 2026. This joint military campaign transcended previous limited engagements, systematically targeting Iran's ballistic missile infrastructure, naval assets, and critical command-and-control nodes across the provinces of Tehran, Isfahan, Qom, Karaj, and Kermanshah. The operational climax of this campaign was the successful decapitation strike on the Pasteur Street district in central Tehran, which resulted in the assassination of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei within his heavily fortified compound.The elimination of the Supreme Leader, alongside the deaths of high-ranking military officials including Armed Forces Chief of Staff Abdolrahim Mousavi, fundamentally shattered the theocratic command structure that had governed the Islamic Republic since 1979. In the immediate aftermath, the state’s apparatus attempted to project continuity by forming an Interim Leadership Council. However, the reality on the ground was one of profound systemic failure. The national economy, already crippled by years of maximum pressure sanctions, hyperinflation, and the depreciation of the rial, entered a state of terminal freefall. Furthermore, the civilian population, deeply traumatized and radicalized by the brutal suppression of the 2022 "Woman, Life, Freedom" protests, seized the opportunity presented by the regime's disorientation to launch the most geographically expansive anti-government uprising in the nation's history, spanning over 185 cities.Navigating this catastrophic power vacuum is President Masoud Pezeshkian. Elected in 2024 following the sudden death of his hardline predecessor Ebrahim Raisi in a helicopter crash, the 69-year-old Pezeshkian is a reformist politician and a former cardiovascular surgeon. Historically, Pezeshkian operated within the strict confines of the Nezam (the ruling system), advocating for internal reforms, ethnic minority rights, and diplomatic engagement with the West, while maintaining public deference to the Supreme Leader and the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). However, the unprecedented destruction wrought by the 2026 war fundamentally altered his strategic calculus. Recognizing that the surviving hardline elements of the IRGC intended to establish a fully militarized dictatorship to prolong a suicidal war of attrition, Pezeshkian determined that the survival of the Iranian nation necessitated the total dismantlement of the Islamic Republic.This predictive geopolitical audit models a specific, high-stakes wargame scenario: "The Reformist Coup & Constitutional Abolition." The simulation projects the precise strategic staging, tactical mechanics, psychological warfare, and factional blowback involved as President Pezeshkian leverages the chaos of the bombardment to execute a self-coup. Driven by the urgent need to meet Washington's preconditions for a total ceasefire and prevent the "Syrianization" of Iran, Pezeshkian moves to publicly abolish the office of the Supreme Leader, suspend the 1979 Constitution, and initiate an immediate transition toward a secular parliamentary republic.1. The Staging Ground & The BroadcastThe Room: The Academic Redoubt at the University of TehranThe execution of a broadcast announcing the dissolution of the Islamic Republic constitutes an act of high treason under the existing penal code. Consequently, the selection of the staging ground is of paramount strategic, tactical, and symbolic importance. In standard operational procedures, a presidential address of this magnitude would be transmitted from the Islamic Republic of Iran Broadcasting (IRIB) headquarters, located in the affluent northern Tehran neighborhood of Velenjak.However, the IRIB complex was neutralized as a viable asset during the initial phases of Operation Epic Fury. The Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) identified the IRIB—specifically its iconic, modernist "Glass Building" designed by Abdol-Aziz Farmanfarmaian—as a critical command node utilized by the IRGC to advance military operations under the guise of civilian broadcasting. Following an evacuation warning, Israeli fighter jets deployed dozens of precision munitions, striking the facility mid-broadcast. The bombardment inflicted severe structural damage on the V-shaped concrete and steel edifice, igniting fires that destroyed the fourth-floor newsroom and executive offices. In the aftermath, heavily armed IRGC intelligence operatives locked down the Jame Jam compound, transforming the smoldering ruins into a restricted military zone and rendering it inaccessible to the reformist President.Deprived of state media infrastructure, Pezeshkian is forced to secure an alternative location that offers both physical defensibility and profound symbolic resonance. The simulation identifies the subterranean levels of the Faculty of Law and Political Science at the University of Tehran as the optimal staging ground. Located on Enghelab (Revolution) Avenue in the heart of the capital, the Faculty was established in 1899 as the School of Politics and has historically served as the intellectual crucible for Iran's modern legal and political frameworks. More crucially, the University of Tehran has been the epicenter of student resistance, playing a pivotal role in the 1999 student uprisings, the 2009 Green Movement, and the 2022 protests. By broadcasting from this specific location, Pezeshkian explicitly aligns his coup with the historic struggles of the Iranian intelligentsia and the youth demographic.The atmosphere within this makeshift broadcast center is defined by controlled chaos and acute vulnerability. The campus perimeters are hastily fortified. Wrought-iron gates are chained shut, and barricades constructed from overturned vehicles, concrete planters, and academic furniture block the main arteries leading to the university. Inside the basement of the Law Faculty, the environment is far removed from the polished aesthetics of a state address. The air is thick with the smell of ozone, stale sweat, and the electrical burn of hastily spliced servers. Portable diesel generators hum loudly, powering a localized intranet and encrypted satellite uplinks smuggled onto the campus by sympathetic telecommunications engineers to bypass the regime's nationwide internet blackout.Student activists, wearing makeshift tactical gear, particulate masks, and carrying improvised weapons, act as an outer perimeter defense. The immediate corridors surrounding the broadcast room are secured by defecting regular army commandos. The lighting is harsh and uneven, provided by battery-powered halogen work lights that cast long shadows against the bare concrete walls. Whiteboards, ordinarily used for jurisprudence lectures, are covered in frantic logistical schedules, casualty reports, and maps of IRGC troop movements. Pezeshkian sits at a scarred wooden desk, his suit jacket discarded, projecting the stark visual austerity necessary to reinforce the desperate, revolutionary nature of the broadcast.The Announcement: Forensic Honesty and the Terminal DiagnosisMasoud Pezeshkian’s political persona is inextricably linked to his professional background as a specialized cardiovascular surgeon and a former Minister of Health. He is widely recognized by the Iranian public for his rhetorical style of "forensic honesty"—a clinical, unembellished approach to diagnosing the nation's socio-economic and political pathologies. In this scenario, his broadcast must achieve two monumental tasks simultaneously: it must systematically dismantle the theological legitimacy of the Velayat-e Faqih (Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist), and it must justify his extra-constitutional seizure of power to a traumatized, war-weary populace.Pezeshkian's address leverages his medical authority to frame the Islamic Republic not merely as a flawed political system, but as a biological organism suffering from multi-organ failure. He addresses the camera with the solemn, measured cadence of a senior physician informing a family that all palliative options have been exhausted. The core theme of the declaration is that the theocracy is a "terminal patient." He argues that decades of ideological rigidity, systemic corruption, and the violent suppression of the nation's own youth have triggered a fatal autoimmune response within the Iranian body politic."For forty-five years, we have treated the symptoms of our national decline with the painkillers of ideology and the bandages of state violence," Pezeshkian declares, looking directly into the lens. "But the underlying disease has metastasized. The body of Iran is in septic shock. The institutions that were built to protect this nation have become the very pathogens destroying it."He explicitly names the office of the Supreme Leader, the unelected Guardian Council, and the hardline clerical establishment as necrotic tissue that has poisoned the bloodstream of the nation. Using stark surgical imagery, he explains that just as a surgeon must amputate a gangrenous limb to prevent an infection from reaching the heart, the Iranian state must excise its theocratic institutions to survive the current geopolitical trauma.Pezeshkian formally declares the 1979 Constitution suspended, citing its inability to protect the fundamental life functions of the citizenry. He announces the formation of an emergency secular transitional authority, directly appealing to the United States, Israel, and the international community by stating that the ideological apparatus driving regional proxy wars and nuclear brinkmanship has been surgically removed. By framing the dissolution of the republic as an objective medical necessity rather than a partisan political preference, he attempts to neutralize theological counter-arguments. The coup is presented not as a power grab, but as the only viable life-saving intervention for a nation on the brink of total annihilation.2. The Mechanics of the Coup (The Intervention)The Muscle (The Ragtag Coalition)Despite his constitutional title as President, Pezeshkian is structurally weak. The Iranian presidency has historically been subordinate to the absolute authority of the Supreme Leader and the sprawling military, intelligence, and economic empire of the IRGC. The IRGC was explicitly designed by Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini as an ideologically pure parallel force to coup-proof the regime and safeguard the revolution from internal dissent. To physically hold the University of Tehran, enforce his decrees, and survive the inevitable assassination attempts, Pezeshkian must rely on a volatile, ad-hoc coalition of disaffected military elements and highly mobilized civilian networks.The primary kinetic force backing the coup originates from the regular armed forces, the Artesh. Since the 1979 revolution, the Artesh has been systematically marginalized, underfunded, and treated with deep suspicion by the clerical establishment, which consistently favored the IRGC in budget allocations and procurement. The widespread destruction of IRGC command nodes by US and Israeli strikes, coupled with the systemic failure of the IRGC's air defense networks during Operation Epic Fury, has provided the Artesh with a unique strategic window to assert dominance and reclaim its status as the premier national military force.Specifically, the simulation identifies the Artesh's 65th Airborne Special Forces Brigade—commonly known as the NOHED Brigade or the "Green Berets"—as the tip of Pezeshkian's spear. Established in 1959 and originally trained by US Special Forces advisors prior to the revolution, the 65th Airborne is Iran's most elite and capable conventional special operations unit. The brigade specializes in unconventional warfare, hostage rescue, airborne insertion, and psychological operations. Historically deployed in the Dhofar Rebellion in Oman and extensively utilized during the Iran-Iraq War, the unit possesses a deeply ingrained nationalist ethos that contrasts sharply with the extraterritorial religious zealotry of the IRGC's Quds Force. Clad in their distinctive DPM camouflage and equipped with modern tactical gear, elements of the 65th Airborne defect to the transitional government, establishing an impenetrable inner ring of security around the university and actively neutralizing IRGC intelligence operatives attempting to infiltrate the campus.Complementing this professional military vanguard is the Neighborhood Youth Alliance of Iran (also known as the United Youth of Iran). Forged in the crucible of the 2022 Mahsa Amini protests, this decentralized, cellular network of underground youth groups operates across dozens of Iranian cities. Operating clandestinely to evade the regime's surveillance apparatus, the Alliance utilizes platforms like Telegram and secure VPNs to mobilize participants and coordinate strikes. Their ideological foundation is cemented in a 43-article manifesto published in December 2022, which explicitly calls for the overthrow of the Islamic Republic, the establishment of a secular democratic government, full gender equality, and adherence to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.In the wargame scenario, the Youth Alliance acts as Pezeshkian's localized intelligence apparatus and outer defensive shield. They mobilize tens of thousands of students, labor activists, and disenfranchised citizens to flood Enghelab Avenue and the surrounding streets, creating a massive human buffer. This civilian mobilization serves a dual purpose: it visually legitimizes Pezeshkian's transition to the international community, and it severely complicates any heavy armored assault by surviving IRGC loyalists, who would be forced to commit mass atrocities on live camera to reach the university gates.The US Counter-Demand: The Proof of LifeThe mere broadcast of a secular transition is vastly insufficient to satisfy Washington or Tel Aviv. Given the long history of Iranian strategic deception, the resilience of the IRGC's deep state, and the sheer scale of the ongoing US military deployment, the Trump administration demands immediate, highly visible, and irreversible kinetic actions to prove that Pezeshkian exercises actual operational control, rather than serving as a temporary figurehead broadcasting from a besieged basement. The US explicitly requires "proof of life" for the reformist coup before it will authorize a cessation of the bombing campaign.The primary US counter-demand focuses on the physical breaching and liberation of Evin Prison. Located at the foot of the Alborz mountains in the Evin neighborhood of northern Tehran, the facility has served as the ultimate symbol of the Islamic Republic's repressive apparatus for decades. The prison is the primary site for incarcerating political dissidents, journalists, academics, human rights lawyers, and dual-national hostages used for diplomatic leverage.The US specifically mandates the liberation of two highly secure sections of the complex: Ward 209 and Ward 2A. Ward 209, controlled exclusively by the Ministry of Intelligence (MOIS), consists of ten rows of solitary confinement cells, often severely overcrowded, and features subterranean interrogation rooms equipped with medieval torture devices, including the infamous "Apolo" bed used for administering electric shocks. Ward 2A is under the direct control of the IRGC Intelligence Organization, operating entirely outside the purview of the standard judicial system. Both wards are characterized by deliberate medical neglect, prolonged solitary confinement, and the systematic extraction of forced confessions, which are frequently broadcast on IRIB.Pezeshkian must order the 65th Airborne Brigade's Hostage Rescue Unit (Unit-110) to execute a daring, daylight assault on Evin Prison. The commandos must breach the outer perimeter, overwhelm the IRGC and MOIS guards, navigate the labyrinthine corridors of Wards 209 and 2A, and physically liberate the detainees. This operation, broadcast live via satellite uplinks provided by the Neighborhood Youth Alliance, would irrevocably shatter the regime's aura of invincibility, dismantle its primary coercive tool, and fulfill a core US human rights objective, thereby securing international recognition for the transitional government.Simultaneously, the US demands the apprehension and handover of key ideological architects of the regime's domestic terror apparatus. The primary high-value target designated in the simulation is Ayatollah Ahmad Alamolhoda. Alamolhoda is the ultra-conservative Friday prayer leader of Mashhad, a prominent member of the Assembly of Experts, and the representative of the Supreme Leader in Khorasan Razavi province. Furthermore, he is the father-in-law of the late President Ebrahim Raisi, cementing his position at the apex of the hardline elite.Alamolhoda is infamous for his virulent rhetoric and uncompromising enforcement of ideological purity. He has repeatedly issued religious decrees justifying the lethal suppression of protesters, advocating for the violent enforcement of mandatory hijab laws, and demanding the execution of reformist figures under charges of moharebeh (enmity against God). He has also frequently called for the destruction of Israel and the mobilization of Muslims for global jihad. The extraction of Alamolhoda from his provincial stronghold in Mashhad by defecting Artesh units, and his subsequent handover to an ad-hoc international tribunal for crimes against humanity, would serve as a definitive decapitation of the hardline clerical establishment. This action would provide incontrovertible proof to Washington that the structural pillars of the Islamic Republic's domestic oppression and regional aggression have been permanently dismantled.3. The Civil War Twist (Dynamic Injects)The Rogue Faction (The Hardline Backlash)The simulation dictates that the abolition of the theocracy and the dismantling of the Islamic Republic will not go unchallenged. Despite the assassination of the Supreme Leader and the destruction of centralized command nodes, the regime's security architecture was specifically engineered to survive decapitation strikes and wage asymmetric warfare. The most immediate and lethal threat to Pezeshkian’s fragile reformist coalition emanates from the Paydari Front (Front of Islamic Revolution Stability) and the decentralized networks of the Basij paramilitary.The Paydari Front represents the ultra-conservative, eschatological wing of the Iranian political spectrum, deeply embedded within the IRGC, the judiciary, and state media organs. Led by figures such as Sadegh Mahsouli and heavily influenced by the radical teachings of clerics like Ayatollah Mohammad-Mehdi Mirbagheri, the party promotes an apocalyptic interpretation of Shiism centered on the return of the Mahdi. Rejecting any form of diplomatic compromise, nuclear concessions, or internal reform, the Paydari Front views Pezeshkian’s broadcast as the ultimate betrayal of the 1979 revolution and an existential threat to their ideological vision.Utilizing the IRGC's "Mosaic Doctrine"—a structural reorganization implemented in 2008 that decentralized command and control to 32 provincial units to ensure survival against foreign invasion or massive internal unrest—hardline commanders immediately activate sleeper cells and local militias. Operating out of heavily fortified provincial mosques, which serve as dual-purpose religious centers and decentralized armories, Basij commanders mobilize the Ashura (male) and Al-Zahra (female) rapid response battalions, which were specifically established to suppress urban uprisings.The ideological counter-attack is swift and uncompromising. This religious decree effectively legalizes his assassination and commands all devout personnel within the armed forces to turn their weapons against the transitional government.
