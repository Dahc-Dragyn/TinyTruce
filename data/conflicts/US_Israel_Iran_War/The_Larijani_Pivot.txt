The Larijani Pivot: A Forensic Geopolitical Audit of Iranian Regime Collapse and Strategic CapitulationIntroduction: The Crucible of Operation Epic FuryIn March 2026, the geopolitical architecture of the Middle East underwent a seismic rupture following the execution of Operation Epic Fury (U.S.) and Operation Lion’s Roar (Israel). This unprecedented, coordinated military campaign culminated in the targeted decapitation of the Islamic Republic of Iran's apex leadership, including the death of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. The strikes heavily degraded the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) command structure and devastated the highly secured Pasteur Street leadership compound in central Tehran, traditionally the neural center of the Iranian theocracy.In the immediate vacuum created by the elimination of the Supreme Leader, the state apparatus confronted an existential crisis, caught between total structural collapse, an apocalyptic military escalation, and rapid internal fragmentation. Against this backdrop of paralyzing uncertainty, a predictive wargame scenario emerges: "The Larijani Pivot." In this matrix, Ali Larijani—a veteran pragmatist, long-time Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC), former IRGC commander, and the regime's foremost strategic diplomat —orchestrates a soft military coup. Bypassing the newly formed, inherently fragile Interim Leadership Council , Larijani sidelines the hardline clerical establishment and the radical elements of the IRGC. Recognizing the mathematical impossibility of surviving continued U.S. and Israeli air superiority , he initiates an unconditional surrender to the United States to preserve the physical continuity of the Iranian nation-state.This exhaustive geopolitical audit extracts the structured narrative, operational mechanics, theological frameworks, and dynamic threat injections required to populate a high-fidelity wargame simulation of this regime-collapse scenario.1. The Staging Ground & The BroadcastThe Room: Subterranean Command, Saadabad Palace ComplexWith the total destruction of the Pasteur Street compound—a sprawling, multi-street complex that previously housed the Presidential Office, the Assembly of Experts, and the SNSC war room —Larijani's operational footprint must shift to a survivable, highly secure location capable of projecting state authority. He realistically stages his soft military coup and broadcasts his instrument of surrender from the deep underground bunkers beneath the Saadabad Cultural-Historical Complex in northern Tehran.Physical and Atmospheric Details:
Originally established during the Qajar and Pahlavi dynasties as a 110-hectare summer royal residence nestled in the Shemiran area at the foothills of the Alborz Mountains , the Saadabad complex overlays a labyrinthine subterranean network. Following the revolution, these bunkers were retrofitted by the IRGC and the Ministry of Intelligence (MOIS) to serve as a continuity-of-government redoubt.The atmosphere in the bunker is distinctly post-apocalyptic, stripped of the Islamic Republic's traditional opulence. The exquisite, hand-woven Persian carpets that once lined the upper palatial floors have long been removed—reportedly looted during previous administrative power struggles involving IRGC factions —leaving bare, high-density reinforced concrete designed to withstand the shockwaves of advanced penetrator munitions. The air is heavy with the scent of ozone from overworked encrypted communication terminals and the acrid tang of diesel exhaust venting from auxiliary generators, a necessity given the systematic degradation of Tehran's power grid by U.S. electromagnetic warfare and cyber-attacks.The visual framing of Larijani’s broadcast is meticulously calculated to signal a definitive rupture from theocratic absolutism. In the bunker's central war room, Larijani sits at the head of a massive, scarred mahogany table. Unlike his hardline predecessors who traditionally appeared in clerical robes or IRGC olive-drab fatigues to project revolutionary zeal , Larijani is dressed in a sharp, dark, open-collared suit. He projects the austere, clinical authority of a technocratic statesman navigating a national catastrophe. Flanking him are not the bearded clerics of the Guardian Council, but stoic, highly decorated generals of the regular armed forces (the Artesh), clad in formal military dress. Conspicuously absent from the camera's frame are any portraits of Ayatollah Khomeini or Khamenei. Instead, the backdrop features only the simple, unadorned tri-color flag of Iran, subtly reorienting the visual narrative from the preservation of the "Islamic Revolution" to the survival of the ancient "Iranian Nation."The Announcement: Weaponizing Pragmatism for State SurvivalThe core strategic challenge for Larijani is bridging an impossible cognitive dissonance: he must frame an unconditional surrender to the "Great Satan" not as a humiliating defeat, but as the ultimate act of Iranian patriotism and an Islamically justified necessity. Drawing upon his unique academic background as a PhD in Western philosophy (with a focus on Immanuel Kant), his early career as an IRGC officer, and his tenure as the architect of the 2015 JCPOA nuclear negotiations , Larijani’s address fuses cold geopolitical calculus with theological realism.Core Themes of the Initial Address:The Supremacy of State Survival (Hefz-e Nezam): Larijani anchors his capitulation in the foundational jurisprudence of Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini, who famously decreed that the preservation of the Islamic State is the highest religious duty, superseding all other Islamic obligations, including prayer and fasting if necessary. Larijani argues that continuing an unwinnable, asymmetrical war against the absolute technological and aerial supremacy of the U.S. and Israel  guarantees the total annihilation of the Iranian people. He frames the surrender not as a loss of faith, but as the painful, religiously mandated amputation of a gangrenous limb to save the living body of Iran.The Illusion of "Forward Defense" and Proxy Betrayal: Larijani systematically dismantles the regime's long-standing regional strategy. He points out that the billions of dollars funneled into the "Axis of Resistance"—Hezbollah in Lebanon, Hamas in Gaza, and the Houthis in Yemen—failed entirely to protect Tehran from decapitation strikes. He frames the hardline IRGC's reliance on these decentralized militias as a strategic delusion that bankrupted the Iranian economy, invited foreign bombs onto sovereign soil, and ultimately left the nation defenseless when the true war arrived.The Pivot from Ideology to Civilizational Nationalism: Larijani enacts a sharp rhetorical pivot toward Persian nationalism, appealing to the thousands of years of Iranian civilizational history. He asserts that while governments, leaders, and even revolutions pass into history, the land and civilization of Iran must endure. He accuses radical clerics and rogue IRGC elements of prioritizing an endless, apocalyptic death cult over the lives, prosperity, and future of 90 million Iranians, effectively branding the hardliners as the true enemies of the state.The Burden of True Military Leadership: Speaking directly to the armed forces, Larijani invokes his own pedigree as an early IRGC commander. He validates the courage of the rank-and-file soldiers but declares that true military leadership requires the bravery to recognize when the tactical geometry of the battlefield is mathematically fatal. He issues a direct order as the de facto head of state for all forces to stand down, framing the immediate ceasefire as a "strategic retreat" required to rebuild a shattered nation.2. The Mechanics of the Coup (The Intervention)The Muscle: The Artesh and Pragmatist Intelligence FactionsLarijani possesses immense political capital, deep institutional knowledge, and vast familial networks across the judiciary and parliament. However, a soft coup against a deeply entrenched, hyper-militarized hardline apparatus requires kinetic force. The IRGC, numbering roughly 125,000 personnel and backed by the vast Basij paramilitary network, operates as the regime's ideological praetorian guard, commanding the nation's ballistic missiles and holding a stranglehold over up to 60% of the Iranian economy. To physically neutralize the hardline leadership, Larijani must engineer an unprecedented operational alliance between the conventional military and pragmatist factions within the intelligence community.The Artesh Vanguard:
Historically, the regular armed forces (the Artesh) have been systematically sidelined, underfunded, and viewed with deep suspicion by the clerical establishment, which heavily favored the ideologically pure IRGC. Following the devastating losses suffered by the IRGC in the June 2025 "12-Day War" and the subsequent March 2026 decapitation strikes, a strategic window opens. Larijani secures the covert backing of Major General Abdolrahim Mousavi (Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces) and Major General Amir Hatami (Commander-in-Chief of the Army). Embittered by decades of IRGC hubris and the catastrophic failure of the IRGC's air defense networks , the Artesh leadership agrees to deploy its elite conventional commando units, such as the 65th NOHED Brigade, to secure critical state infrastructure, including the state broadcasting center (IRIB), major transportation hubs, and the telecommunications grid.The MOIS Intervention:
The Iranian intelligence landscape is dangerously fractured between the civilian-led Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS) and the parallel IRGC Intelligence Organization. MOIS is generally populated by more analytical, pragmatic, and technocratic operatives who have frequently clashed with the IRGC's heavy-handed domestic crackdowns and aggressive extraterritorial operations. Larijani utilizes loyalist networks within MOIS to silently jam IRGC command-and-control communications, blind the IRGC's domestic surveillance matrices, and track the real-time movements of surviving hardline elites.The Arrests:Utilizing Artesh special forces guided by precise MOIS targeting data, Larijani initiates a wave of synchronized, bloodless raids aimed at the newly formed Interim Leadership Council and its shadow backers.Target One - Ayatollah Alireza Arafi: Arafi, the staunchly loyalist cleric appointed as the jurist member of the Interim Council , is detained at his residence in the seminary city of Qom. He is placed under heavily guarded house arrest by Artesh forces, effectively severing the clerical establishment's legal mechanism to govern the transition.Target Two - Mojtaba Khamenei: The most critical target is the slain Supreme Leader's 56-year-old son, Mojtaba Khamenei. Despite lacking formal religious rank, Mojtaba wields immense influence through the "Habib Circle," operating as a shadow ruler with deep, unbreakable ties to the IRGC's senior command. The IRGC had heavily lobbied the Assembly of Experts to install Mojtaba as the new Supreme Leader to ensure hardline continuity. MOIS operatives intercept Mojtaba's convoy in a secure district of Tehran, detaining him at an undisclosed safehouse.By neutralizing Arafi and Mojtaba Khamenei, Larijani paralyzes the Assembly of Experts, preventing them from legally confirming a hardline successor and leaving Larijani to operate with uncontested, de facto executive authority.The U.S. Counter-Demand: The 24-Hour Verification WindowIf Larijani offers an unconditional surrender to halt the impending destruction of the Iranian state , Washington and Tel Aviv will view the offer with profound skepticism. Aware of Iran's two-decade history of nuclear deception and the deep subterranean nature of its enrichment facilities , the U.S. requires immediate, undeniable proof that Larijani genuinely controls the nuclear apparatus and is not merely buying time for a rogue IRGC faction to execute a nuclear breakout.The Immediate Demand:
Within the first 24 hours of Larijani's broadcast, the U.S. issues a non-negotiable ultimatum: the physical surrender, verification, and extraction planning of Iran's entire stockpile of 60% Highly Enriched Uranium (HEU), estimated at over 400 kilograms prior to the strikes. Furthermore, Larijani must definitively prove he has neutralized the IRGC's "Command for the Protection and Security of Nuclear Centres".The Humiliating Verification Step:
Larijani must grant immediate, unrestricted, and unannounced "anytime, anywhere" access to joint teams comprising International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) inspectors and embedded U.S. Special Operations Forces (SOF) operating under military protocols for securing foreign nuclear sites, specifically Joint Publication 3-40 (Countering Weapons of Mass Destruction) and JP 3-41 (CBRN Response).These specialized teams are to be airlifted directly into the heart of the Islamic Republic's most sensitive installations, including the Natanz complex and the Fordow Fuel Enrichment Plant. Fordow, buried 80 to 100 meters beneath solid granite , represents the ultimate test of Larijani's control. He must order Artesh units to relieve and disarm the IRGC perimeter guards. U.S. forces will then physically enter the tunnels to tag, seal, and take custody of the advanced centrifuge cascades and the HEU canisters. This physical handover is designed to be a deeply humiliating geopolitical spectacle, broadcast globally by Washington to permanently shatter the myth of Iranian nuclear sovereignty and verify Larijani's total capitulation.3. Allegories of State Survival (Thematic Vocabulary)In the deeply complex Iranian political ecosystem, raw pragmatism cannot be presented nakedly; it must be heavily clothed in theological legitimacy and historical memory. Larijani, a master of Iranian rhetorical architecture, weaponizes specific cultural allegories to justify his treason against the hardliners and sell his capitulation to the broader public.Extraction of Historical and Political MetaphorsThe Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (628 CE): A pivotal 10-year truce signed by the Prophet Muhammad and the pagan Quraysh tribe of Mecca. While it appeared to early Muslims as a humiliating and unfair compromise, the peace allowed the nascent Islamic community to survive, gather strength, and eventually conquer Mecca without mass bloodshed. Larijani uses this to frame surrender as a divinely inspired tactical pause.The Peace of Imam Hassan (661 CE): The second Shi'a Imam, Hassan ibn Ali, signed a controversial peace treaty with the Umayyad caliph Mu'awiya. By choosing peace over a suicidal military confrontation, Imam Hassan prevented the total slaughter of his followers and preserved the bloodline of the Imamate. Larijani invokes this to demonstrate that true, righteous leadership sometimes requires swallowing pride and absorbing humiliation to save the Ummah."Heroic Flexibility" (Narmesh-e Ghahremananeh): A modern political concept introduced by Ali Khamenei himself in 2013 to justify entering into the JCPOA nuclear negotiations. It uses the metaphor of a wrestler tactically stepping back in the ring to find a better angle of attack. Larijani brilliantly turns Khamenei's own words against the hardliners, claiming his actions are the ultimate fulfillment of the late Leader's strategic vision.Drinking the Poisoned Chalice (Jām-e Zahr): A profound metaphor made famous by Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini in 1988 when he reluctantly accepted UN Security Council Resolution 598, ending the brutal eight-year Iran-Iraq War without achieving absolute victory over Saddam Hussein. It symbolizes a leader taking on the bitter, agonizing burden of compromise for the sake of the nation's survival. Larijani casts himself as the martyr drinking the poison to save the state.The Treaty of Turkmenchay (1828): A deeply traumatizing historical memory in Iran. The Qajar dynasty was forced to sign this treaty after a crushing military defeat by the Russian Empire, permanently ceding vast territories in the Caucasus (modern-day Armenia and Azerbaijan). Hardliners use this as the ultimate negative metaphor, equating Larijani's surrender to the greatest treason in Persian history.Mapping Pragmatist Allegories vs. Hardline DogmaTo effectively simulate the internal information warfare of this scenario, wargame parameters must account for how the same operational action is described using violently divergent terminology by Larijani's pragmatists and the IRGC hardliners.Operational ActionLarijani / Pragmatist AllegoryIRGC / Hardline DogmaSigning the Surrender"The Wisdom of Hudaybiyyah" / "Heroic Flexibility""A New Turkmenchay" / "Capitulation to the Great Satan"Ending the War"Drinking the Poisoned Chalice for the Nation""Abandoning the Axis of Resistance" / "Betrayal of the Martyrs"Purging the Hardline Clerics"Removing the Rot from the Cedar" / "Protecting the State from Zealots""War against Velayat-e Faqih" / "Apostasy and Sedition"Allowing U.S. Nuclear Verification"Strategic Transparency" / "Preserving the Infrastructure""Opening the Gates to the Invader" / "Surrendering National Dignity"Arresting Mojtaba Khamenei"Preventing Un-Islamic Hereditary Rule (Monarchy)""Silencing the True Heir of the Revolution"Weaponizing Maslahat against Velayat-e FaqihThe core ideological friction of the Larijani Pivot is his subversion of the Islamic Republic's foundational governing architecture. By arresting the clerics and overriding the IRGC, Larijani claims he is not destroying the Islamic Republic, but rather utilizing Maslahat to save the Iranian nation from a collective suicide cult orchestrated by disconnected zealots. He translates "surrender" into a masterstroke of statecraft, rendering the hardliners' cries of religious treason constitutionally and practically moot.4. The Insurgency Twist (Dynamic Injects)No wargame simulation of Iranian regime change can assume a linear, peaceful transition. The deep state of the Islamic Republic was designed precisely to resist such a coup. Larijani's pivot will inevitably trigger a violent, asymmetrical immune response from the most radicalized wings of the security apparatus.The Rogue Faction: The Saberin Takavar Brigade and Quds Force RemnantsThe primary kinetic threat to Larijani's fragile ceasefire comes not from the conventional military, but from highly specialized, ideologically pure sub-units of the IRGC that operate outside traditional chains of command.The Saberin Unit: The Saberin Takavar Brigade is the elite special operations force of the IRGC Ground Forces. Modeled loosely on Western tier-one units but forged in extreme Khomeinist ideology, the roughly 6,000 to 8,000 operators are handpicked for their absolute, fanatical loyalty to the Supreme Leader. Battle-hardened from extensive combat deployments in Syria, Iraq, and counter-insurgency operations against Kurdish separatists in the Zagros mountains, the Saberin consider Larijani's surrender an act of highest apostasy.Quds Force Remnants and Basij: Specifically, elements of Unit 400 (the Quds Force covert operations branch responsible for extraterritorial assassinations and attacks) and radicalized cells of the Basij paramilitary militia.The "Mosaic Defense" Factor: Crucially, the IRGC operates under a doctrine known as "Mosaic Defense," implemented in 2007 by former commander Mohammad Ali Jafari. This doctrine decentralized the IRGC into 31 semi-autonomous provincial commands designed to wage guerrilla warfare independently if Tehran's central command was ever decapitated. Consequently, these rogue units do not require centralized orders to initiate an insurgency; they are doctrinally pre-programmed to view Larijani's Artesh-backed government as an illegitimate puppet regime and to fight on autonomously.The Nightmare Scenario: Seizure of the Haji Abad SilosAs U.S. forces begin landing at Natanz to verify the nuclear surrender, a catastrophic mid-simulation inject disrupts the geopolitical equilibrium. A rogue battalion of Saberin operators, coordinating with a radicalized local IRGC Aerospace commander, initiates a localized insurgency in the rugged, mountainous terrain of Hormozgan province in southern Iran.The Seized Asset: The rogue faction successfully storms and seizes the Haji Abad underground missile base. Unlike standard road-mobile launchers that can be hunted by U.S. air patrols, the Haji Abad facility features deeply buried, hardened circular silos containing solid-fuel Medium-Range Ballistic Missiles (MRBMs), specifically the Kheibar (Khorramshahr-4). Because these missiles are solid-fueled, they do not require hours of volatile liquid fueling; they maintain a state of near-instant launch readiness. The Kheibar missile carries a massive 1,500kg warhead, utilizes evasive maneuverability (MaRV) to defeat air defenses like Israel's Iron Dome or U.S. Patriot batteries, and possesses a range of 2,000 kilometers.The Sabotage: Rejecting Larijani's ceasefire, the rogue Saberin commander broadcasts a martyrdom manifesto directly to regional proxy networks and initiates the launch sequence for a volley of Kheibar missiles. The targeted coordinates are split to maximize geopolitical chaos: half are aimed at the U.S. Fifth Fleet headquarters in Manama, Bahrain , and half are aimed at civilian population centers in Tel Aviv, Israel. The rogue faction's goal is unambiguous: force the U.S. and Israel to execute a massive retaliatory strike against Iran, thereby collapsing Larijani's peace deal, uniting the fractured Iranian populace against a foreign invader, and igniting a region-wide conflagration that serves their apocalyptic worldview.Immediate Global FalloutThe seizure of the Haji Abad silos by an autonomous, hyper-radicalized IRGC faction generates an immediate, multi-domain geopolitical crisis that alters the parameters of the wargame:The Threat of Escalation: Washington and Tel Aviv are faced with a nightmare calculus. If the Kheibar missiles launch and bypass interceptors, the resulting mass casualties will compel Israel and the U.S. to execute a massive kinetic retaliation. This could escalate to the use of tactical nuclear weapons or necessitate a full-scale ground invasion to secure all remaining missile sites , thereby completely destroying Larijani's fragile transitional government and triggering the exact war he sought to prevent.Economic Shockwave: The realization that heavily armed, suicidal IRGC factions control solid-fuel MRBMs directly overlooking the Persian Gulf sends global energy markets into a tailspin. Brent crude prices shatter historical ceilings as insurers refuse to underwrite tankers navigating the Strait of Hormuz, effectively choking off 20% of the world's oil supply and threatening global recession.The Credibility Crisis and Civil War: Larijani’s authority evaporates instantly on the global stage. The U.S. immediately halts the nuclear verification process, realizing that Larijani cannot guarantee the security of Iran's most dangerous infrastructure. Larijani is forced to order the Artesh Air Force to bomb its own countrymen at Haji Abad to prevent the launch, risking a full-blown, multi-factional civil war within the borders of Iran.Proliferation Panic: Global intelligence agencies panic at the precedent. If rogue IRGC units can easily seize MRBM silos and bypass internal safeguards, they could theoretically seize undisclosed radiological materials, chemical weapons, or biological agents. This transforms a localized regime collapse into an immediate, transnational counter-terrorism emergency, forcing neighboring states to drastically alter their defensive postures.ConclusionThe "Larijani Pivot" scenario highlights the profound volatility inherent in the collapse of complex authoritarian systems. While the prospect of a pragmatist coup and unconditional surrender offers a fleeting illusion of geopolitical stability, the deeply embedded institutional duality of the Iranian state—specifically the ideological fanaticism and decentralized architecture of the IRGC—ensures that any transition will be violently contested. By forcing wargame participants to navigate the treacherous space between Larijani's high-stakes diplomacy, the U.S.'s demanding verification protocols, and the Saberin Brigade's apocalyptic insurgency, this matrix provides a highly realistic audit of the cascading crises that would follow the fall of the Islamic Republic.
